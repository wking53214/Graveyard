## The big picture

So the cleanest answer is:

**34 current repositories/assets in the architectural universe**, but **far fewer than 34 should be considered "the ArnoldAI product."**

I'd actually model ArnoldAI as:

```text

   COGNITION            GOVERNANCE           EXECUTION
```

**That is the important distinction:** ArnoldAI isn't one repo. **It's the architecture that gives these repos meaning and interoperability.**
