### And there's one major thing we should NOT do

We shouldn't now start connecting every repository to every other repository.

The purpose of the forensic reconstruction is to determine:

Your question about HERALD is exactly the right example.

The global graph should ultimately distinguish:

```text
SOLID LINE   = verified runtime/data dependency
DASHED LINE = verified conceptual/provenance relationship
DOTTED LINE = historical lineage
RED LINE     = prohibited/bypassed path
```

That would give you the **actual system-of-systems graph**, rather than a diagram that simply assumes everything belongs together.
