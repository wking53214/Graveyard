### One important change from Pass 1

I deliberately made **Pass 2 historical rather than architectural**.

Pass 1 asked, essentially, *“What does the GitHub evidence say exists?”*

Pass 2 asks, *“How did it get here, and what did you actually intend at each point?”*

That distinction is important because otherwise the research model will be strongly tempted to look at the present-day **TIE/GEMS/HERALD/CCC/Triad-42/Sentinel/Conservation** constellation and reverse-engineer a neat architecture that may never have existed as a deliberate design.

Also, I included **all of the repository URLs that matter to the reconstruction**, including the historical/unavailable ones and the newer **HERALD, TIE, KAGGLE, and ghost_tools** repositories that appear in your Pass 1 material. This gives the researcher an explicit universe rather than letting it silently decide which repos are "important."
