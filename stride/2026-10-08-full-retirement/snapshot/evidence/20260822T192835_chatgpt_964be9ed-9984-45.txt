## FINAL RULE

**Never convert convergence into ancestry.**

If multiple systems eventually enter GSA-815, FORTRESS, or GSA-GATEWAY, that proves convergence only.

If two archives describe the same system, that is corroboration only unless the evidence establishes identity.

If an artifact has the same filename in two archives, that is not enough.

If two artifacts contain distinctive matching code, that is strong evidence.

If one archive says another system migrated into a repository, treat that as a claim until independently supported.

If evidence is missing, write **U**.

If the archive is demonstrably incomplete, write **N/U — archival coverage limitation**.

If two pieces of evidence conflict, preserve the conflict and investigate it.

**Do not resolve uncertainty by intuition.**

The final consolidated history should therefore be a **layered, evidence-weighted provenance graph**, not a retrospective narrative that makes the ecosystem appear cleaner than the surviving evidence actually permits.
