# PASS 11 — GEMINI_HISTORY TEMPORAL ARCHAEOLOGY
## Deep historical recovery, date-range excavation, and graveyard-system reconstruction

Audit date: August 22, 2026
Method: primary-source temporal archaeology
Standard: skeptical historian / software archaeologist
Priority: chronology > naming > repository structure

==================================================
CRITICAL CONTEXT
==================================================

TIE, GEMS, HERALD, and Conservation_Kernel are BRAND-NEW systems created during the August 2026 build/reconstruction period.

DO NOT attempt to establish historical ancestry for them.

They are irrelevant to the historical-origin investigation.

The purpose of this pass is to go BACKWARD through Gemini history, potentially into APRIL 2026 and earlier, to identify the actual evolution of the older ecosystem.

This history may contain systems that:

- no longer exist;
- were renamed;
- were deleted;
- were merged;
- were replaced;
- only existed temporarily;
- survive only as conversation-generated code;
- survive only as attachments;
- later contributed artifacts to Sentinel, GSA-815, GSA-GATEWAY, FORTRESS, or other systems.

This may be the deepest temporal layer in the entire investigation.

==================================================

- the system was renamed.

Temporal conflicts are evidence, not errors to be erased.
