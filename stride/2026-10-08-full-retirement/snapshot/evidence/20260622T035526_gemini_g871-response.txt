Yes, they each have distinct architectural roles and unique functional specializations. While they share the goal of deterministic text enforcement, they occupy different conceptual layers within your system architecture. 
 1. FACTS (Forensic Audit & Clinical Transformation System) 
 Unique Aspect: Forensic Integrity & Normalization 
FACTS is primarily a sanitization engine designed for data-at-rest or data-in-transit normalization. Its core unique value is the deterministic generation of a forensic integrity manifest . 
 
 Key Focus: It acts as a final "purger" that strips sociological drift (pronouns) and qualitative bias (subjective adjectives). 
 The "Clinical" Angle: It is built for a deterministic pipeline where the final output—complete with a SHA-256 hash—must be mathematically verifiable. It treats text as an audit trail. 
 
 2. STRIDE (Secure Telemetry Runtime and Intelligence Deterministic Engine) 
 Unique Aspect: Orchestration & Gateway Policy 
STRIDE is a runtime gateway architecture . It is not just about cleaning text; it is about governing it within a high-concurrency, asynchronous environment. 
 
 Key Focus: Telemetry, backpressure management, and cryptographic attestation. It manages a worker pool ( asyncio.Queue ) and performs real-time policy assessment using a "zero-trust" filter gate. 
 The "Intelligent" Angle: STRIDE computes operational risk, monitors system volatility, and orchestrates multi-attempt feedback loops. It is designed to run in a continuous, live production environment. 
 
 3. Citadel (Deterministic LLM Output Enforcement Engine) 
 Unique Aspect: Transformative Compliance & Scoring 
Citadel is a policy-enforcement engine specialized in linguistic quality control. Its unique aspect is the combination of detection, scoring, and automated rewriting . 
 
 Key Focus: Compliance profiles ( ops , exec , legal ). It doesn't just block or pass; it computes a granular violation score and applies context-specific transformations (e.g., simplifying passive voice or removing hedging). 
 The "Compliance" Angle: It is built as a library for fine-grained enforcement, allowing you to tune the "strictness" of the output based on the specific audience or regulatory requirement.
