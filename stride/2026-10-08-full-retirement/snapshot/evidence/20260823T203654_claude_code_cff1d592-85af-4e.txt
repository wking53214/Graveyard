# HERALD

**The natural language interpretation layer.**

A herald carries a message. A herald does not decide what the message means,
and a herald has no authority over what happens next. That is the whole
design, and the name is the shortest statement of it.

---

## What problem this solves

Free text is the choke point. Every project in the stack that touches
unstructured language currently handles it ad hoc, unsupervised, and
differently. HERALD is the one place that work happens, so it can be done
once and reviewed once.

## What it will not do

It will not decide anything. HERALD turns text into **candidate claims** and
hands them to the consuming project's own governance layer. It cannot be the
final arbiter of a legal conclusion, an eligibility call, an adverse action,
a protected-class inference, a compliance verdict, a risk score, or an
authorization. Those are decisions. This layer does not decide.

That is not a convention. It is enforced in code: a claim whose kind names a
governed determination raises `BoundaryViolation` at construction. See
[CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md).

=== CITADEL ===
# CITADEL

CITADEL v1.1 is a deterministic LLM output enforcement engine — regex-based
detection of first-person pronouns, hedging language, passive voice, and
em-dashes, with a scoring/rewriting pipeline (`CitadelDetector`,
`CitadelTransformer`, `CitadelScorer`, `Citadel`). See `PROVENANCE.md` for
the full source history and `TRANSCRIPT.md` for the original audit
conversation.

`citadel_v1.2.py` adds one further check on top of v1.1: `prohibited_verbs`,
salvaged from the STRIDE repo before its deletion, flagging vague
corporate-speak verbs (`improve`, `optimize`, `enhance`, `leverage`, etc.).
Nothing else changes from v1.1 — same classes, same profiles, same scoring
model — the new constraint is wired consistently into `Constraint`,
`PROFILES` (enabled for `ops`, off elsewhere), `PENALTY`, `detect()`, and
`rewrite()`.

## Related repo

[STRIDE](https://github.com/wking53214/STRIDE) is a gateway system whose
source explicitly includes "Zero-Trust Citadel Linguistic Interceptors" as
a component — STRIDE's gateway appears to consume this repo's enforcement
logic as a sub-component.
