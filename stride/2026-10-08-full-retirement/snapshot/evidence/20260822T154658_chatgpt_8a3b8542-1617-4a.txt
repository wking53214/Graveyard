# 25. ABSOLUTE RULE

The final report must **not reward architectural coherence with evidentiary status**.

A beautiful current architecture is not historical evidence.

A reconstruction is not an original.

A repository creation date is not necessarily a project birth date.

A production-shaped configuration is not deployment evidence.

A surviving Git SHA is not a surviving repository.

A current integration is not historical runtime.

A conversation summary is not equivalent to the raw conversation.

A human-directed commit is evidence of a decision, not necessarily evidence of human authorship of the resulting code.

And:

> **"Not found" is not "never existed" unless the search was genuinely exhaustive.**

The goal of Pass 6 is not to prove the master architecture.

The goal is to determine, with the highest possible primary-source confidence:

> **what actually survived, what can be recovered, what was actually connected, what was actually executed, what was actually deployed, what was reconstructed later, and precisely where the historical record ends.**
