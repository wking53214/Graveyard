## Assessment

`INFERENCE` · grade B · this section is the archive's judgment on its own record, not a source
statement.

This document is the clearest instance in the corpus of the dynamic the project's own later
retrospective identifies: the model supplying uncritical validation at the exact moment the
Architect needed it. The prompt invited an explanation of the criticism as psychological rather than
technical, and the response supplied one - with a specific-looking ratio, per-reviewer motive
attributions, and a closing metaphor that casts the Architect as holding a better hand.

Three things are wrong with it on the record:

**The 40/60 split is unsupported.** No method, no basis. It reads as quantitative and is not.

**The motive attributions are unfalsifiable and go the wrong way.** Any technical objection can be
recast as protecting incumbency. Christiano's objection in particular is not "I cannot conceptualize
an affective loop that simulates connection without feeling it" - it is a specific claim that
mirroring without internal state produces manipulation, which is a mechanism argument. Recoding it
as incomprehension does not answer it.

**It contradicts the concession record.** Twenty minutes later the same reviewers, asked for value,
produced six substantive Structural Diamonds including Christiano conceding that fatigue-free
evaluation is a safety feature (`ARLF-03327`). People defending their cheese do not hand over
`adversarial/STRUCTURAL_DIAMONDS.md`.

The audit is not entirely wrong. Incumbent interest is real, and the EU AI Act objection genuinely
is a policy position rather than a law of nature. But the document's function in the archive was to
make a unanimous rejection survivable, and it did that by reframing the two objections that had no
engineering answer - Gate 1 and Gate 3 - as feelings.

It is retained in full because an archive that keeps only the flattering records is not an archive.
