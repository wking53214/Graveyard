## The finding

The record's own synthesis:

> The exposure of the full Citadel shifts the experts' responses from tactical critique to
> existential rejection. They recognize that the VSA is not designed to be an AI product competing in
> their market; it is designed as a succession event meant to replace the market entirely.

This is the diagnostic value of the exposure. In isolation ARLF drew engineering objections. In
context it drew political ones, and the same isolation that had looked like a safety property looked
like a mechanism of control. Nothing about ARLF changed between the two passes; only what the
reviewers could see around it.
