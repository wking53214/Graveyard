# PROVENANCE POLICY

The raw Gemini export is immutable and is not copied into this repository. Every claim points to a
source record index and an evidence ID of the form `ARLF-NNNNN`, where `NNNNN` is the zero-padded
record index in `original_gemini_export.json`.

Missing metadata is `UNKNOWN`. It is never inferred silently. Where this repository infers
something from record structure - such as which field holds the Architect's prompt - the inference
is stated in `METHODOLOGY.md`.

Four provenance statuses are in use:

- `HISTORICAL_STATEMENT` - the source record says this. The grade attaches to the fact that it was
  said, not to whether it is true.
- `RECONSTRUCTION` - this repository assembled the statement from cited source records.
- `INFERENCE` - this repository drew a conclusion the source does not state. Requires an explicit
  basis and cannot be graded A.
- `PROPOSAL` - original design work written after the archive, addressing a gap the archive records.
  Confined to `proposals/`. Takes no evidence grade, because it makes no claim about the corpus. It
  may cite the corpus for the gap it addresses, and those citations are graded normally.

Simulated content is labeled. The corpus contains extensive expert critique attributed by name to
real researchers, executives and regulators. That content was generated by a language model roleplaying
those people. It is recorded here because the archive records it and because it drove real
decisions in the project, never as a representation of anything those people said.
