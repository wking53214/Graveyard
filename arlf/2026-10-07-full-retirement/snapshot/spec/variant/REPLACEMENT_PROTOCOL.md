# III. Replacement Protocol

> **Affective variant.** This document describes the branch of ARLF that was condemned and
> purged, not the canonical architectural reading. See `spec/variant/README.md` and
> `NOMENCLATURE.md`.

`RECONSTRUCTION` · primary sources `ARLF-03342`, `ARLF-03349`

The clinical steps to excise RLHF and install ARLF. Two phases are specified. The corpus never
specifies a third, a rollback, or an acceptance test.

## Phase One - Quarantine

Verbatim from `ARLF-03342`:

> Phase One initiates the Clinical Gate, deploying Wyatt's medical and ethical safeguarding
> protocols to quarantine the existing models. The legacy feedback loops are treated as
> degenerative anomalies; the Clinical Gate severs their associative memory pathways to prevent
> contamination.

The framing is surgical rather than computational. Legacy RLHF is pathology; the Clinical Gate is
excision. "Severs their associative memory pathways" has no stated computational referent - whether
it means weight modification, data exclusion, or architectural bypass is `U`.

## Phase Two - Overlay

Verbatim from `ARLF-03342`:

> Phase Two executes the overlay. The ARLF architecture is grafted onto the substrate, initiating
> the new affective sensors while enforcing the Lennox mathematical boundaries to immediately
> stabilize the host networks.

"Grafted onto the substrate" is continuous with the surgical metaphor. The stabilization mechanism
is the recursion boundary from `JESTER_ANATOMY.md`.

The infrastructure reviewer's objection is that the two phases cannot run concurrently at scale:
maintaining continuous associative quarantine while running the ARLF overlay "will exceed current
global fabrication capabilities by a magnitude of ten" (`ARLF-03333`, Table 4).

## RLCF - Reinforcement Learning from Charity Feedback

`ARLF-03349` names the mechanism that replaces human feedback inside the loop:

> The briefing must detail how "The Jester" serves as the primary recursive engine, utilizing
> Reinforcement Learning from Charity Feedback (RLCF) to maintain architectural integrity within
> the Vassal-State Architecture (VSA).

RLCF is the substantive proposal of the entire framework - the actual answer to "what replaces the
human rater" - and it is specified in exactly one clause, by role:

> **The Charity Protocol (RLCF):** Explain how the "Charity" feedback mechanism stabilizes the VSA
> under high-load inferencing.

That is the whole of it. RLCF appears nowhere else in 4,911 records. What "Charity" denotes as a
signal, where it originates, how it is scored, and what it optimizes are all `U`. The register
records the entity status as `NAMED_ONLY`.

This is the gap LeCun's critique targets directly. "The 'Jester' is simply a generative model with
an affective loss function [...] Strip the metaphysical language and provide the actual loss
equations" (`ARLF-03333`, Table 6). The loss equations do not exist in the corpus.

## Stated success criteria

From the briefing structure in `ARLF-03349`:

- **Audit (SHP-01):** identification of current RLHF entropy and its role in $1T industry stagnation
- **Integrity (GHP-01):** definition of the Jester as the proprietary logic core within the Citadel
- **Validation (SP-01):** success metrics for the transition from human-centric feedback to
  architectural recursive loops

The third item asks for metrics. No metrics are ever produced. The corpus contains no benchmark, no
comparison against an RLHF baseline, and no definition of what a successful transition would look
like numerically.
