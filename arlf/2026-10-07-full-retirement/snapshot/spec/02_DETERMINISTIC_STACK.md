## Layer 5 and Volume 4: the stack forbids the variant

Layer 5 neutralizes simulated empathy. Volume 4 blocks simulated care outright and caps affective
mirroring below 9%. Volume 5 forbids the machine from mimicking personhood.

Those three provisions prohibit, by specification, exactly what the affective branch of ARLF was
built to do. The realized architecture of the architectural reading is partly constituted by the
exclusion of the affective reading.

"Empathy Weapon" is the Black Paper's own section title for its ARLF critique (`ARLF-03324`, section
II). The successor architecture took the critique's vocabulary as the name of its countermeasure.
Inversion is grade A; that these components were built *in response to* ARLF is not stated in the
corpus and is graded B. See `lineage/edges.jsonl`.

## Documented gaps: the Ghost Problems

`ARLF-01575` audits four unresolved friction points. All four bear on the architectural reading, and
two are direct consequences of the determinism claim.

**The Authority Gap.** The load-bearing one:

> When the rigid truth gates encounter complex, high-entropy data, the system risks entering an
> infinite "Loop of the Absurd" because it lacks a clear protocol to handle valid unknowns safely.

A stack that incinerates whatever fails to resolve to a binary has no defined behavior for a valid
unknown. This is the price of Volume 1's 1.000 Parity requirement, and it remains open in the record.

A proposed resolution, written in 2026-09 and not sourced from the corpus, is at
`proposals/AUTHORITY_GAP_RESOLUTION.md` with a runnable implementation under
`proposals/reference/`. It is new work carrying no evidence grade.

**Quantitative Calibration of Humor.** The sub-9% Joy Dial constraint "lacks a definitive
mathematical proof," fluctuating between sterile dryness and sarcastic sycophancy. The component
enforcing affective containment is itself uncalibrated.

**Grid Decoupling.** Running locally on off-network low-power hardware passes sandbox tests, but a
portable "Moral Seal" has not been achieved; the architecture still requires cloud-level compute to
audit low-power local logic.

**Ethical Priority Overlaps.** No tie-breaker protocol when structural justice and human choice
conflict in real time.

Taken together the Ghost Problems are the honest core of the document. A framework claiming
deterministic compliance records, in its own audit, that it cannot yet define what to do with a
question that has no determinate answer.

Two of the four now have proposed fixes in `proposals/`, and two do not. Grid Decoupling and Ethical
Priority Overlaps remain open with nothing offered against them.
