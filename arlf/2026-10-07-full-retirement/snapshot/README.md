# ARLF

Reconstruction of the **ARLF** framework at its peak documented capacity, assembled from primary
activity records in the Gemini archive.

ARLF was a proposal to remove the human from the machine-learning feedback loop. It split into two
readings sharing one acronym: an affective branch that its own review panel destroyed within three
hours, and an architectural branch that was never condemned, was built out into a seven-layer
deterministic compliance stack, and outlived the other. This repository recreates both.

## What ARLF was

**Canonical reading: Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback.** The replacement of probabilistic,
human-supplied training signal with deterministic internal structural verification. Where RLHF asks a
human whether an output is good, ARLF asks the architecture whether an output is compliant. The corpus
names the substitution directly: "replaces 'Probabilistic Correctness' with 'Axiomatic Compliance.'"

A recursive engine called **The Jester** acts as the logic core, verification runs against an isolated
sovereign reference called **The Root**, and the named feedback mechanism is **RLCF**, Reinforcement
Learning from Charity Feedback.

**The affective variant**, *Affective Recursive Loop Framework*, closes the same loop on physiological
and sentiment data converted to mathematical gradients. It is the version that was specified in full,
submitted to review, condemned unanimously, and purged as a systemic hazard. It is preserved in
`spec/variant/`.

Why the architectural reading is treated as canonical, and what that determination costs, is set out in
`NOMENCLATURE.md`. **No executable implementation of either reading appears anywhere in the corpus.**

## Peak capacity

**2026-06-04T06:03:09Z**, the White Paper v1.0 doctrinal statement:

> The document details the transition from traditional, probabilistic RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from
> Human Feedback) to a deterministic, structural approach called ARLF (Architectural Recursive Logic
> Feedback).

That record carries the full 7-Layer No-Trust Stack, five specialized volumes, the validation
apparatus, the operating envelope, and a four-item audit of the framework's own unsolved problems. It is
also the last record in which ARLF is live doctrine rather than a historical entry, five days after the
affective branch was purged. The rejected candidate dates, including the previously selected
2026-04-14 institutional peak, are in `reports/PEAK_CAPACITY.md`.

## Evidence discipline

Every claim resolves to a source record index in the immutable Gemini export. Reconstructed prose is
marked `RECONSTRUCTION`; source statements are marked `HISTORICAL_STATEMENT` and graded. A grade-A
record is grade A only for what that record itself establishes. See `EVIDENCE_POLICY.md` and
`PROVENANCE_POLICY.md`.

The choice of canonical reading is editorial, is argued rather than asserted, and the superseded
determination is kept rather than deleted.

Nothing here asserts that ARLF worked, was buildable, or should be built. It asserts what the archive
says was specified, argued, and decided.
