internal verification, which split into two readings sharing one acronym: an affective branch that its
own review panel destroyed within three hours, and an architectural branch that was never condemned,
was realized as a seven-layer compliance stack, and outlived the other by specification.**

## The three-sentence version

ARLF proposed that the human rater be removed from the loop, and offered two incompatible replacements
under one name: read the human's body and emotions instead, or stop asking about quality and check
compliance against fixed axioms. The first was specified in a single pass, submitted to six domain
reviewers, and condemned unanimously as an empathy weapon, a legal impossibility and a physical one.
The second was never reviewed, was built out into a seven-layer deterministic stack with five
specialized volumes and a documented list of its own unsolved problems, and is the reading the
architecture still carried after the first was purged.

## Findings

**1. The acronym names two different proposals, and only one was destroyed.** Four of six critiques and
four of six Black Paper vectors require physiological sensing to land. The architectural reading is
the half nobody condemned. Whether the split was salvage or drift is `U`. See `NOMENCLATURE.md`.

**2. Six of seven structural seeds survive the removal of the affective gradient**, four as named
components of the realized stack. The seed that does not is Picard's, which supplied the affective
mechanism and needed mathematics its source work does not provide. See `spec/03_STRUCTURAL_SEEDS.md`.

**3. The successor architecture implements one reading and forbids the other.** Layer 5 neutralizes
simulated empathy, Volume 4 blocks simulated care and caps affective mirroring below 9%, Volume 5
forbids mimicked personhood. The surviving reading is partly constituted by excluding the purged one.
Inversion grade A, causation grade B. See `spec/02_DETERMINISTIC_STACK.md`.

**4. The affective branch's architecture and its condemnation describe the same machine.** All six
properties offered as strengths in Sovereign Integrity return as the Black Paper's six disaster
vectors, one to one, with nothing changed but the sign. The review did not dispute what it would do.
See `spec/variant/SOVEREIGN_INTEGRITY.md`.

**5. Only the presentational objection was acted on.** Of four Hard Gates, the project addressed the
jargon: the secularization pass renamed the Lintel of Provenance to the Logic Root for patent and
venture purposes. The loss equations the review demanded were never produced. See
`adversarial/HARD_GATES.md`.

**6. The architectural reading's own gap is RLCF.** Reinforcement Learning from Charity Feedback is the
substantive answer to what replaces the human rater. It is specified in two clauses by role, appears
nowhere else in the corpus, and carries entity status `NAMED_ONLY`. The affective branch at least named
its signal source; the architectural branch names a mechanism and leaves it empty. See
`spec/01_MECHANISM.md`, and `proposals/RLCF_SPECIFICATION.md` for a proposed fix that is new work
rather than reconstruction.

**7. The determinism claim has a recorded cost.** A stack that incinerates whatever fails to resolve to
a binary has no defined behavior for a valid unknown. The corpus records this as the Authority Gap and
leaves it open. It is the most substantive open problem in the framework. See
`proposals/AUTHORITY_GAP_RESOLUTION.md` for a proposed fix that is new work rather than
reconstruction.

**8. The salvage was real but smaller than claimed.** Six Structural Diamonds were conceded; four are
Black Paper condemnations restated positively. Only recursive determinism and hierarchical
world-modeling stand independently, and both make no reference to affect, which is to say both belong
to the architectural reading. See `adversarial/STRUCTURAL_DIAMONDS.md`.

**9. The adversarial method was sound; one record subverted it.** Seating the review council outside
the architecture under the Glass Wall Protocol is good practice and produced the Black Paper. The bias
audit two minutes after the burn-down reframed the two unanswerable objections as psychology, with an
unsupported 40/60 ratio. Both records are retained. See `adversarial/BIAS_AUDIT.md`.

## What this repository does not claim

That either reading would have worked, or could be built. That the divergence between the two readings
was intentional. That the reviewers were right or wrong about anything beyond what the records show
them saying. That the named researchers, executives and regulators hold the views attributed to them:
those are simulated personas, and that attribution is `U`.

## Findings that postdate the archive

`proposals/` contains original design work addressing findings 6 and 7, with a tested reference
implementation. It is not part of the reconstruction, carries no evidence grade, and does not close the
historical gaps: both were open in 2026 and the record continues to say so.
