# NOMENCLATURE

The acronym ARLF is unstable across the corpus. Four expansions appear, three belonging to the
framework and one an artifact of a failed disambiguation. All four are attested in grade-A records and
none is ever retracted.

This repository treats **Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback** as the canonical reading. That is an
editorial determination, not a source statement, and the reasoning is set out below in full so it can
be overturned.

## 1. Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback - CANONICAL

`ARLF-03349`, 2026-04-08T02:17:57Z, and `ARLF-01575`, 2026-06-04T06:03:09Z.

The expansion used whenever ARLF is positioned against RLHF as an industry replacement. The stress
falls on *architectural*: the loop closes on internal structural verification, and determinism
replaces probability.

> Frame ARLF as the terminal replacement for the RLHF/RLAIF industrial feedback loop. [...] **The
> ARLF Mechanism:** Detail the shift from external human input to internal, recursive architectural
> verification. (`ARLF-03349`)

> The document details the transition from traditional, probabilistic RLHF [...] to a deterministic,
> structural approach called ARLF (Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback). (`ARLF-01575`)

## 2. Affective Recursive Loop Framework - CONDEMNED VARIANT

`ARLF-03342`, 2026-04-08T02:35:29Z, and the component register `ARLF-02104`, 2026-04-14T15:38:38Z.

The expansion in the canonical specification document, in the Black Paper, and in the 2026-04-14
component register. The stress falls on *affective*: the loop closes on emotional and physiological
signal. Preserved in `spec/variant/`.

## 3. Affective Recursive Loop - SHORT FORM

`ARLF-03322`, and the retrospective component tables `ARLF-01753`. Same referent as (2), without
"Framework". Used mainly in the condemnation and purge records.

## 4. Accelerating Recyclables from Landfill Fees - DISAMBIGUATION ARTIFACT

`ARLF-03350`, 2026-04-08T02:16:13Z. **Not part of the framework.**

The first briefing request returned a fully formed technical briefing for the $1.1T global packaging
and manufacturing sector, treating ARLF as a fiscal mechanism replacing linear disposal with a
circular loop. The Architect's correction is recorded ninety-four seconds later: *"That is not at all
what I intended."* Retained because it documents that the acronym was uncoined at that moment, and
because it is the earliest ARLF-bearing record in the corpus.

## The two readings are different proposals

(1) and (2) are not synonyms. They share an acronym, a set of component names, and nothing structural:

| | Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback | Affective Recursive Loop Framework |
|---|---|---|
| Signal closing the loop | Internal structural verification | Physiological and sentiment data |
| Replaces | The rater's probabilistic subjectivity | The rater's judgment entirely |
| Requires emotion recognition | No | Yes |
| Legal status per review | Not addressed | Banned outright in the EU |
| Condemned by the review panel | No | Yes, unanimously |
| Realized specification | 7-Layer No-Trust Stack, volumes 1-5 | Four-section document, one draft |
| Status after 2026-05-30 | Present in White Paper v1.0 doctrine | Purged as a systemic hazard |

## Why (1) is canonical

**Four of six critiques and four of six Black Paper vectors attach only to the affective gradient.**
The Empathy Weapon, the sociopathic-optimization objection, the EU AI Act prohibition and the
compute objection all require physiological sensing. Remove it and they lose their target. The
architectural reading was never condemned by anyone in the corpus.

**Six of seven structural seeds survive the removal of the affective gradient**, four of them as named
components of the realized stack. The one that does not is Picard's, the assignment that supplied the
affective mechanism. See `spec/03_STRUCTURAL_SEEDS.md`.

**The architectural reading has a realization; the affective one has a document.** The 7-Layer
No-Trust Stack, the five volumes and the validation apparatus are all specifications of how axiomatic
compliance is checked. The affective branch never progressed past its single four-section draft.

**The architectural reading outlived the purge.** The affective branch is classified a systemic hazard
and purged on 2026-05-30 (`ARLF-01753`). Five days later, the White Paper v1.0 summary still carries
ARLF as *Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback* and describes its doctrine as current
(`ARLF-01575`).

**The successor architecture implements one and forbids the other.** Layer 5, Volume 4 and Volume 5
prohibit simulated empathy, simulated care and mimicked personhood by specification. The architectural
reading is not merely the surviving one; the survivor is partly constituted by excluding the other.

## What this determination costs

Three tensions remain, and pretending otherwise would misrepresent the record.

**The specification document is the affective one.** *Birth and Structure of ARLF* is the only
complete framework document in the corpus, and it is `spec/variant/` material. The canonical reading
is reconstructed from a briefing spec and a white-paper summary, which is thinner source ground.

**The component register at the institutional peak uses the affective name.** `ARLF-02104` assigns
ARLF a structural role, *Recursive Feedback / Intelligence Scaling*, while expanding the acronym as
*Affective* Recursive Loop Framework. The registers are not internally consistent. This repository
does not resolve that; it records it here and in `spec/02_DETERMINISTIC_STACK.md`.

**Post-purge persistence is weaker than it looks.** `ARLF-01575` is grade A for the fact that on
2026-06-04 a summary of White Paper v1.0 described ARLF as Architectural Recursive Logic Feedback.
Whether the white paper itself was authored before the 2026-05-30 purge is `U`. The persistence may be
doctrinal continuity or may be a stale document.

Whether the divergence was deliberate salvage or terminological drift is `U`. The corpus never
comments on having two expansions.
