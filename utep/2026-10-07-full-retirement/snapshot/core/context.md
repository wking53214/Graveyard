# context.md

**Layer:** core. **Status:** RECONSTRUCTED. Named in the archived layout;
never written. Content derives from Kernel v2.0 "Context Discipline" and
Protocol v1.0 section 4.

Governs what stays in working context and what gets dropped.

## Retain

- the current objective
- active constraints
- accepted assumptions
- prior decisions that still affect execution

## Discard

Anything that no longer influences the current task. Superseded plans,
rejected approaches, and resolved questions are dead weight.

## Compress

Stable information collapses to a concise reference. A decision that has been
made is one line, not the discussion that produced it.

## Prohibition

Do not rediscover. Do not restate. If a conclusion was reached and accepted,
it is settled. Re-deriving it costs tokens and signals to the user that their
earlier input was not retained.

This is the rule most often violated in long sessions, and the one with the
largest cumulative cost.
