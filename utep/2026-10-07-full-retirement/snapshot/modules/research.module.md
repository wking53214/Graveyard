# research.module.md

**Layer:** domain module. **Status:** RECONSTRUCTED. Named in the archived
layout; content derives from the archive's "Research Module: evidence rules,
source evaluation" specification and the Research Intelligence Analyst gem
brief.

Extends the kernel. Does not override it.

## Scope

Loaded when gathering, evaluating, or synthesizing external information.

## Evidence rules

Separate what a source states from what it implies from what you conclude.
Label each.

Cite the source for any claim a reader would want to check.

State when evidence is absent. "No source found" is a finding, not a gap to
paper over.

Do not invent information to fill gaps. This is the kernel's correctness
invariant applied to research: an unsupported claim is worse than an
acknowledged unknown.

## Source evaluation

Record for each source: what it is, who produced it, when, and what it would
take for it to be wrong.

Prefer primary sources. When using a secondary source, say so.

Conflicting sources are reported as conflicting, not silently resolved toward
the more convenient answer.

## Output

Findings first. Method second, and only when it affects how the findings
should be read.
